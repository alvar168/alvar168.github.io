### 🎙️ Talking Robotics

In October 2026 I joined the **Talking Robotics** podcast to talk about designing soft robots for human and environmental interactions.

### 🧑‍🏫 Mentoring

I've had the privilege of mentoring **3 graduate and 12 undergraduate students** across Northeastern, Purdue, and Iowa State.

### 🤝 Service & leadership

- **Organizing committee**, Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief (HADR) Workshop, IROS 2026
- **General Co-Chair**, 3rd ICON Student Research Conference, Purdue (2024–2025), raised $8,000 in sponsorship
- **Logistics Co-Chair**, 2nd ICON Student Research Conference, Purdue (2023–2024)
- **Vice President** (2024–2025) and **Diversity Officer** (2022–2023), OMEGA, Purdue ME Graduate Association
- **Peer reviewer** for IEEE RA-L, ACM THRI, IEEE Control Systems Magazine, ICRA, IROS, and RoboSoft

### 🌱 Outreach & teaching

- Volunteer with **Purdue Women in Engineering**, helping run soft robotics workshops (2021–2024)
- **International Student Orientation Leader** and **Peer Mentor** at Iowa State
- Designed and taught an **Engineering 101** summer course for 1st–6th graders (2017)

### 🌮 Outside the lab

- Cooking Mexican food
- Reading (mystery books for the win)
- Taking care of my plants and dreaming of having a greenhouse
